QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the free water test visit?

Our expert technician visits your location to test water quality including pH level, TDS, hardness, and contamination levels. Based on the results, we recommend the best suitable system for your needs. This service is completely free with no obligations.

Is installation free?

Yes! Professional installation is completely free with every system purchase. Our trained technicians will install and test your system to ensure it works perfectly before handover.

What warranty is provided?

All products come with 1 year comprehensive warranty covering parts and manufacturing defects. We also offer paid maintenance and AMC services for long-term protection and optimal performance.

Do you offer bulk discounts for apartments and companies?

Yes! We have special bulk purchase schemes for apartments, construction companies, and large projects. Contact us for customized pricing based on your requirements and order quantity.

Can I upgrade or modify my existing system?

Absolutely! We provide paid maintenance services and can modify or upgrade existing systems. Whether you have a manual, semi-automatic, or automatic system, we can enhance it based on your changing needs.

What's the difference between automatic, semi-auto, and manual systems?

Automatic systems run with minimal intervention, semi-automatic requires some manual adjustments, and manual systems need complete manual operation. All offer the same water quality but differ in convenience and pricing.

Do you serve all areas in Bangalore and Karnataka?

Yes! We serve all major areas across greater Bangalore including Whitefield, Koramangala, Indiranagar, and all other regions in Karnataka. Call us to confirm service availability for your area.
