    How often do filters need replacement?

    Filters typically need replacement every 6-12 months depending on water quality and usage. We provide annual maintenance plans to handle all replacements.

    Is installation included in the price?

    Yes, professional installation is completely free. Our trained technicians will set up your system and ensure it's working perfectly before we leave.

    What warranty do you offer?

    All products come with a 5-year comprehensive warranty covering parts and labor. Extended warranties are available for additional protection.

    Do you provide emergency maintenance?

    Yes, we offer 24/7 emergency support. Contact our helpline and our team will respond within 2 hours for urgent issues.

    Can the system be upgraded later?

    Absolutely! Our modular systems can be upgraded to higher capacity models. We'll provide a trade-in value for your existing unit.
