Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a water softener and an RO plant?

A water softener removes hardness minerals (calcium, magnesium) using ion exchange, making water safe for washing and cleaning. An RO plant removes all dissolved solids including salts and minerals, making water ideal for drinking.

How often do I need to refill salt in my water softener?

Depending on your water hardness and usage, you typically refill salt every 4-8 weeks. Our automatic softeners have high-capacity tanks, and we provide convenient salt delivery services.

Is the free water testing really free?

Yes! Our water testing is completely free. Our technician will visit your home, test your water for hardness, TDS, pH, and bacteria, and provide a detailed report with customized solutions.

How long does installation take?

Most water softeners and RO plants are installed in 2-4 hours. Commercial systems may take longer depending on complexity. We ensure zero water wastage and proper disposal of old systems.

Do you provide maintenance and service?

Yes! We offer annual maintenance packages, emergency repairs, spare parts, and filter replacements. Our 24/7 support team is just a WhatsApp away.

What warranty do you provide?

All our products come with manufacturer warranty (typically 1-2 years). We also offer extended warranty and maintenance packages for long-term peace of mind.
